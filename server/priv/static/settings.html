Your comfort

At most four incoming vibrations in 24 hours, at least 90 minutes apart. No catch-up after a pause. Incoming ripples stay silent for the first 24 hours. With Pond 0.1.3 or later, previews and your optional confirmation tick work immediately in Gentle mode, outside quiet hours and pauses.

Room for quiet

Uses your watch’s local time. Equal start and end times turn scheduled quiet hours off; “Quiet all day” silences the whole day.

Your pebble

How Pond works

Try two short, quick wrist twists—a quick back-and-forth motion—to prepare a drop. It feels more like a wrist twist than a screen tap in this build. Repeat the gesture within two seconds to cancel. Pond detects motion, not screen touches; no need to hit the glass. A drop made while a fresh shared ripple is visible answers that bundle.

“Dropped” means the service accepted it. It does not promise that another person felt it. Gestures and haptics are experimental in this first build.

Privacy & service

No account, location, health data, messages, or activity analytics. Your pebble and comfort settings stay on your devices.

Contributions and eligibility records expire after 24 hours. Inactive installations expire after 30 days. Leaving deletes linked live data; anonymous bundles already delivered expire naturally.

Operators must configure seven-day encrypted backups and sanitised logs before public use. This development server does not configure those automatically.

Use HTTPS. Localhost HTTP is for development. Leave the current pond before joining another host; changing a host does not delete its old data.
