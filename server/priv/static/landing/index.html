01 / YOU SAY HELLO

A little wrist twist.
A little plop.

Try two short, quick wrist twists—a back-and-forth motion—to prepare a drop. This early build detects motion, not screen touches. No need to bonk the glass. Changed your mind? Repeat within two seconds to cancel. Rocks understand.
